This Privacy Policy explains how VITTAL MIRJI (we, us, our) collects, uses, shares, and protects personal data when you use vitthalmirji.com (Website) or purchase services.

Technical consulting services. No physical goods. No restricted categories.

1. What we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Identity and contact info: name, email, phone, company, role
  • Communication content: emails, messages, meeting notes you send us
  • Billing details: invoice references, payment status, partial payment identifiers (we do not store full card details)
  • Technical data: IP address, browser/device info, approximate location, pages visited (via cookies/analytics if enabled)

2. How we use your data

We use personal data to:

  • Provide consulting services and deliver agreed work
  • Create invoices, confirm payments, and maintain accounting records
  • Respond to requests and support queries
  • Prevent fraud/abuse and secure our Website
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Payments and payment processors

Payments are processed through a payment gateway/aggregator (for example, Cashfree & PayU) and the banks/payment networks involved in processing. The payment processor handles sensitive payment credentials as per its systems and policies.

We may receive payment confirmation details such as transaction reference, amount, date, and status.

4. Legal basis and your rights

We process personal data for lawful purposes such as providing services you request, and where required, based on your consent.

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have rights including:

  • correction/updating and erasure (subject to legal retention needs)
  • readily available means of grievance redressal MeitY

5. Data sharing

We may share limited personal data with:

  • Payment processors (for example, Cashfree & PayU and associated banking/payment partners) for payment processing
  • Service providers we use to run the business (hosting, email, analytics, invoicing/accounting), under appropriate safeguards
  • Authorities, if required by law or to protect rights/safety

We do not sell your personal data.

6. Data retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for:

  • delivering services
  • resolving disputes
  • complying with legal/accounting requirements

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data. No method of transmission or storage is 100 percent secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Cookies

We may use cookies to improve site functionality and understand traffic. You can control cookies through your browser settings. If you disable cookies, some parts of the site may not work as intended.

9. Children

This Website and Services are not directed to individuals under 18. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

10. Contact and grievance

For privacy requests or grievances, contact:

  • Privacy Contact: VITTAL MIRJI
  • Email: vitthalmirji@gmail.com
  • Mobile: +91-9845-630-306

We aim to respond within a reasonable time and as required by applicable law. MeitY

11. Updates

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be posted here with the updated date.
